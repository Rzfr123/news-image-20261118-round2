A photo-led newspaper front page for Wednesday 18 November 2026, in the
photojournalism style of CNN / Al Jazeera's coverage of the day.
Entry: https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118

Lead: Trump lands in Shenzhen as Xi and Putin meet ahead of the first
US–China–Russia three-way talks. Also: Iran hits a US base in Kuwait and
sites in Bahrain; Ukraine strikes the Kerch Bridge again; Democrats take
the US Senate 56–44; the Board of Peace warns the Gaza truce is at risk.
Briefs: Ebola passes 11,000 cases in DR Congo; Anthropic raises a record
$81.3bn; the US loses measles-free status after 26 years.

`main.ts` copies the committed `entry.png` (rendered by `render.py` from
the photos in `assets/`, no network) to the output dir and validates it.
